# Link Order Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers the Link Order macOS App Store edition, published by Mesut ARIDORU.

## Information processed on your Mac

Link Order reads network interface information and processes the identities of applications you select, their connection destinations, and traffic byte counts to apply your routing rules. Rules, preferences, and aggregate usage history are stored locally in the application's sandbox or its shared App Group container. Routing operates on your Mac; Link Order does not provide a remote relay or a hosted VPN service.

Link Order does not send application identities, connection destinations, routing rules, or usage history to the developer. It contains no developer analytics, advertising SDK, or tracking service, and does not require a Link Order account.

## Network connections

The applications you route still communicate with the destinations they request. Those destinations and your network or VPN providers have their own privacy practices. Routing through an adapter does not encrypt traffic by itself. Link Order does not claim to control all system DNS requests or to block all traffic if its routing engine stops.

## Purchases

Optional Pro purchases and restoration use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. Apple processes payment information under its privacy policy. Link Order checks the verified purchase entitlement to unlock features and does not receive your payment card information.

## Controls and retention

You can turn off routing rules and remove saved rules or groups. Traffic history is retained locally in minute-level aggregates for 30 days and daily aggregates for up to one year. Resetting a displayed counter changes its baseline; it does not erase the underlying history. Removing all local app data removes saved preferences, routing rules, and usage history. The application and routing extension must both be stopped before removing their containers.

## Support

Support is available at [Link Order support](https://github.com/drony/link-order-releases/issues). GitHub is an external service. Information you voluntarily post in a public issue is public; do not post credentials, personal connection destinations, or private network logs.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before any developer telemetry, account synchronization, or remote relay is introduced. The date above identifies the current policy.
